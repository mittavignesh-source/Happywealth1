Questions.

What do you actually do for me?

We understand your goals and comfort with risk, choose funds that match your time horizon, track your investments and rebalance when needed. No jargon, just clear advice, one plan, one SIP, one step at a time.

How safe is my money with HappyWealth?

We never hold or handle your money. Investments are made in your name and money moves from your bank account to the fund through NSE. Every transaction needs your approval by email or mobile OTP.

You can track the same folios independently on NSE, CAMS and MFCentral. Mutual funds are regulated by SEBI and AMFI.

Why mutual funds over other products?

They're run by experienced fund managers, let you diversify with small amounts, and can be withdrawn fully or partly when you need the money. Professionals handle the portfolio so you don't have to track markets every day.

What's your value addition?

Most long-term success comes down to four things: goal clarity, the right asset allocation, monitoring and diversification. We help you get all four right and stay with them.

How do you earn, and what do you charge?

There's no fee for consultation or advice. We earn a small commission paid by fund houses from the fund's expense ratio, as per AMFI rules. It grows only when your investments grow.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan is like a recurring deposit, except a fixed amount goes into a mutual fund every month by auto-debit. Investing through both ups and downs builds wealth steadily and with discipline.

What are expense ratio and exit load?

The expense ratio is the yearly fee a fund house charges to run the fund. It's already adjusted in the NAV, and all displayed returns are after it.

An exit load is a fee charged only if you redeem within a set period. Long-term investors usually don't pay it.
